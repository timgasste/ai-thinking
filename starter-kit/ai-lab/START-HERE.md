# Start here: your ai-lab folder

This folder goes with the lecture *Why and How to Think with AI*
(timgasste.github.io/ai-thinking).

## What is in it

- `sources/` holds material the agent may read. The lecture slides are already
  here. Add your own notes, readings, or Word files, but only material you are
  allowed to share with an AI tool.
- `outputs/` is where the agent saves what it makes for you.

## Exercise 1: set up your agent

1. Keep this folder anywhere on your computer, for example in Documents.
2. Open the ChatGPT desktop app (a free account works), or another app that
   can work in a folder. Choose **Work** and open this `ai-lab` folder.
3. Paste this task and answer the questions:

   > Interview me with five short questions, one at a time, about my studies,
   > my goals, and how I want you to help me learn. Then save my answers and
   > these rules as INSTRUCTIONS.md in this folder: use only files in this
   > folder, never invent sources, and ask before you overwrite a file.

4. Open `INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Check it, and change one thing yourself.

Prefer Word? Ask for `INSTRUCTIONS.docx` instead. Markdown (`.md`) is plain
text, which models read most reliably, but a Word file works too.

## Exercise 2

Pick one prompt from the lecture page and replace the [brackets] with your own
concept or argument.

Follow the AI rules for your course. This lecture is not a general permission
to use AI in your studies.
